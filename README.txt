# CIMPAL and marIAS

Website for **CIMPAL** index - Cumulative IMPacts of invasive ALien species and **marIAS** – Standardizing knowledge on the Environmental Impacts of Marine Invasive Alien Species.

🌐 Live site: https://cimpal.net

## About
The CIMPAL website was developed under the collaboration between several research projects: 
**MOVIAS** - MOdelling and MOnitoring the MOVement of Marine Invasive Alien Species – Advancing Predictability of Spread and Impacts” (2023-2029) - FCT CEEC Individual Heliana Teixeira Grant: https://doi.org/10.54499/2022.08095.CEECIND/CP1720/CT0017 
**GES4SEAS** - ACHIEVING GOOD ENVIRONMENTAL STATUS FOR MAINTAINING ECOSYSTEM SERVICES BY ASSESSING INTEGRATED IMPACTS OF CUMULATIVE PRESSURES (HEU Grant Agreement 101059877) (2023-2026)
**GUARDIAS** - GUARDING EUROPEAN WATERS FROM INVASIVE ALIEN SPECIES (HEU Grant Agreement 101181413) (2025-2027)
