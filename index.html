CIMPAL

An index to assess the Cumulative IMPacts of invasive ALien species (IAS)
originally developed by Stelios Katsanevakis, Heliana Teixeira and Fernando Tempera (2016)
and recently updated with new features and allowing calculations at higher spatial resolutions, through alternative CIMPAL index calculators.

The CIMPAL family of indices allows not only to assess the negative environmental impacts of IAS, but also their positive impacts, and also their impacts on Ecosystem Services, positive and negative. In addition, a recently expanded version of CIMPAL allows also to calculate the cumulative impacts caused by IAS, Harmful Algal Blooms (HABs) and Jellyfish Blooms.

Several people contributed to these advances and we thank them all: Heliana Teixeira, Julien Radoux, Fábio L. Matos, Gabriel Pelouze, Stelios Katsanevakis, Konstantinos Tsirintanis and Valentini Stamatiadou.

CIMPAL calculators

We implemented alternative tools to compute the CIMPAL index, a user friendly no code needed option and additional workflows offering higher flexibility but requiring minimum coding:

  • 1) CIMPAL GUI calculator in a cloud environment available through LifeWatch ERIC webservices;
  • 2) CIMPAL R-based workflow, available through GitHub, including extra features and adaptable to CIMPAL extended versions.

marIAS Impacts Database

marIAS is a marine Invasive Alien Species impacts database gathering evidence from scientific literature on the negative impacts of IAS in marine biodiversity and ecosystems. It provides ready-to-use information on species-habitat specific impact weights needed to calculate CIMPAL. marIAS is implemented as an online Shiny app.

Biotic Interactions

The new CIMPAL algorithm implementation in R offers the possibility to consider biotic interactions between IAS. Such interaction can have an antagonistic or a synergistic effect on a co-occurring IAS. Biotic interactions evidence between IAS is extracted from the Global Biotic Interactions GloBI database (Poelen et al., 2014).

Requirements to calculate CIMPAL

To assess the cumulative impact of invasive alien species using CIMPAL requires different type of data. Below we outline the main type of information required as well as supporting resources from where some data can be retrieved. A tutorial on how to compile and structure all data files is also available through LifeWatch ERIC.

IAS spatial distribution

Georreferrenced occurrences (presence-absence or abundance) of species.

Habitat maps and zones

Habitats' spatial distribution and zones for aggregation (e.g. administrative boundaries, marine regions).

Impact weights

Magnitude of impact of each species in habitats and associated communities (sensu biotope).

Species Dispersal Radius (optional)

A species-specific mobility trait referring to its daily home range.

Biotic Interactions (optional)

Positive or negative interaction between a given pair of invasive alien species.

Pathways of Introduction (optional)

Main pathway(s) of introdution of a given species.
